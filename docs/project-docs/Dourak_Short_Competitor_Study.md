# Dourak — Short Competitor Study

## Market Summary

The market for digital **جمعية / Savings Circle / ROSCA** apps is real, but most products fall into two groups:

1. **Fintech products that move or hold money**
2. **Organizer / tracking tools that do not touch the money**

For **Dourak v1**, the second model is the better fit.

It is simpler, safer, and easier to launch while still solving the main problem:

> **Organize the circle, track who paid, know whose turn it is, and keep the history clear.**

The strongest market opportunity is not simply “another ROSCA app.” It is:

> **A very simple Arabic-first organizer for private circles that feels as easy as a WhatsApp group, but keeps the financial record structured.**

## Most Relevant Competitors

The closest competitors to Dourak's planned MVP are:

- **Zeed**
- **Fundaloop**
- **Golak**
- **ROSA**
- **juntasonline**

### Zeed

Especially important because it is close to the same audience and workflow:

- Arab-region focus
- Arabic / English
- payment tracking
- reminders
- organizer dashboard
- does not move money

**Dourak opportunity:** be simpler, cleaner, easier for family/friend circles, and stronger in payout-order management.

### juntasonline

Especially important because its **random draw / قرعة** is very close to Dourak's planned workflow.

**Dourak opportunity:** make this feature culturally natural for Arab users, more polished, and easier to share.

### Golak

Strong idea:

> “We never touch your money.”

**Dourak opportunity:** keep the same low-regulation coordination model, but make it much more Arabic-native and simpler.

### Fundaloop / ROSA

Strong on:

- transparency
- accountability
- reliability/history

**Dourak opportunity:** keep the useful tracking ideas, but avoid making the product feel too complex or generic.

### ElGameya / Wafir / Dayrah

These are closer to financial products.

**Dourak opportunity:** avoid competing with them as a bank/fintech product. Focus on private groups that already know and trust each other.

## Dourak v1 — Recommended Focus

Keep v1 narrower and cleaner than most competitors.

- Create a **جمعية / Savings Circle**
- Add members manually or by invitation link
- Set:
  - contribution amount
  - currency
  - start date
  - frequency
- Choose payout order manually
- Or run a **random قرعة**
- Preview and confirm the result
- Lock the order after confirmation
- Generate the full payout schedule automatically
- For every month/cycle show:
  - who paid
  - who did not pay
  - who is late
  - who receives this month
- Confirm payout received
- Member-level history
- Simple reminders
- Arabic + English
- Share a simple monthly status summary to WhatsApp
- **Do not process, hold, or transfer money initially**

This last point is important.

Apps such as Zeed, Fundaloop, ROSA, Golak and SusuWallet show that a useful product can provide coordination and transparency **without touching the money**.

That keeps Dourak focused and dramatically reduces financial/regulatory complexity.

# How Dourak Should Differentiate

## 1. The Best قرعة Experience

Make the draw one of Dourak's signature features.

Suggested flow:

1. Organizer selects eligible members
2. Presses **Start Draw**
3. Dourak generates the order
4. Organizer previews the result
5. Result is confirmed
6. Order is locked

The feature should feel:

- visual
- trustworthy
- simple
- culturally natural
- easy to share

Avoid making it look childish or like gambling.

---

## 2. Extremely Simple Organizer Dashboard

The organizer should open Dourak and immediately see the current situation.

Example:

```text
September

Paid: 7 / 10
Collected: 7,000 / 10,000 SAR
Unpaid: 3

This month's recipient: Ahmad
Payout: Pending
```

The dashboard should answer these questions immediately:

- How much should I collect?
- How much did I collect?
- Who has not paid?
- Who receives this month?
- Was the payout completed?
- Who is next?

No complicated finance screens.

---

## 3. Arab-First Product Design

Use terminology people already understand naturally.

Examples:

```text
جمعياتي
إنشاء جمعية
الأعضاء
القسط
دور الاستلام
القرعة
دفعات هذا الشهر
تم الدفع
لم يدفع
متأخر
تم الاستلام
```

The English UI can use **Savings Circle**, but the Arabic UI should comfortably use **جمعية**.

Do not force formal financial terminology where normal Arabic is clearer.

---

## Product Positioning

Strong positioning:

> **Dourak — Organize your Jam3eya, know whose turn it is, and never lose track of a payment.**

Shorter version:

> **Dourak — Your savings circle, organized.**

Suggested app-store subtitle:

> **Manage Jam3eyas, contributions, turns and payouts.**

---

## What Not to Build Initially

Do not turn Dourak into a fintech product too early.

Avoid in v1:

- holding money
- transferring money
- bank integration
- payment gateway
- wallet
- KYC
- credit scoring
- loans
- investments
- marketplace features
- sophisticated trust score

---

## Trust Score Recommendation

Do not prioritize a trust score in the first version.

A trust score becomes meaningful only when:

- users participate in multiple circles
- identities are reasonably verified
- enough real payment history exists

Instead, first build factual history:

- paid on time
- late payments
- missed payments
- completed circles
- payouts received

Reputation can come later.

# Final Focus

Dourak should win by being:

1. **Simple**
2. **Arabic-first**
3. **Designed for private family/friend circles**
4. **Excellent at the random draw**
5. **Excellent at monthly payment tracking**
6. **Always clear about whose turn is next**
7. **Easy to share through WhatsApp**
8. **A coordination tool, not a bank**

The strongest product idea is:

> **Dourak should feel as easy as managing the جمعية in WhatsApp, but with structure, history, clarity, and no lost information.**
