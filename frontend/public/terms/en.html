What Dourak is

Dourak is an organizing tool for savings circles (جمعية / ROSCA-style groups). It helps a circle's organizer and members track membership, payout order, monthly cycles, and who has reported paying their contribution.

Dourak does not handle money

Dourak does not process, hold, transfer, or guarantee any payment. All actual money changes hands directly between circle members, outside the app, by whatever means they agree on. When a member "reports a payment" in Dourak, that's a self-reported claim for the organizer to verify manually — Dourak has no way to confirm a transfer actually happened, and takes no responsibility if a member misreports a payment or a circle member fails to pay.

Using Dourak to organize a savings circle does not make Dourak a party to any agreement between members, and Dourak is not liable for disputes, unpaid contributions, or losses arising from participation in a circle.
