Dourak Privacy Policy

How Dourak collects, uses and protects your information

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Information we collect

When you create a Dourak account and use the app, we collect:

  • Account information: email address, password (stored as a salted hash — never in plain text), and display name.
  • Profile information (optional): phone number, preferred language, default currency, and time zone, all editable from your profile.
  • Circle and membership data: savings circles (جمعية) you create or join, contribution amounts, currency, start dates, payout order, member lists, cycle schedules, and self-reported payment claims.
  • Usage data: standard technical logs (timestamps, error logs) generated by the app's normal operation, used only to operate and debug the service.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account's email address, name, and profile picture, as shared by Google at the moment you sign in. Dourak never sees your Google password, and never requests access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, or any other Google data beyond basic profile/email.

Dourak does not collect or process actual payments, bank account details, or card numbers — the app only records who reported paying what, for organizers to verify manually.

How we use your information

We use the information above to operate your account, run circle management (schedules, payout order, payment tracking), show each member the status information their role is allowed to see, and keep the service reliable and debuggable.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Sharing with other circle members

Members of a circle can see relevant shared information, such as display names and payment records within that circle. Circle organizers can additionally see a member's contact details (phone/email) for members they manage; ordinary members cannot see each other's contact details.

WhatsApp invitations

Dourak offers an optional way to invite someone to a circle via a WhatsApp share link. Dourak does not access your WhatsApp contacts or messages — the link is generated locally and opened in WhatsApp, which you control.

Data storage and security

Data is stored on servers we control, protected with password hashing (never plain-text passwords) and token-based (JWT) authentication for every request.

Data retention and deletion

Account data is retained for as long as your account remains active. You can delete your own account and its data at any time from the app (Profile → Delete account), or by contacting us at the address below.

Your rights

You may access, update, or request deletion of your personal information at any time, either directly in the app or by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy, or a deletion request you'd rather send by email: anass.shaddad@gmail.com.
