Part 1

Creating the circle and adding members

In this section we'll create a new circle from scratch, add members to it, and learn what to do if one of them doesn't accept.

1

Create a new circle

Press "Create Savings Circle". Enter the circle's name, then each member's monthly contribution amount, the first month's start date, and the day of the month funds are collected (e.g. the 5th).

You don't need to fill in everything now — just the name, amount, and date. You can edit the details later.

2

The circle is created

After pressing "Next" the new circle's page appears. Its status is now "Draft" — meaning it hasn't started yet, and you can still freely edit anything in it.

3

Add members

Press "Add Member", then search for the person by name, email, or phone number, pick them from the list, and press "Add". Repeat this for every member you want to include.

4

Members now show "Pending"

A member you add doesn't join the circle right away — they get a notification and must accept it themselves first. Until they do, their name shows as "Pending".

5

You can't start until everyone has responded

If you try to activate the circle while a member still hasn't responded, a warning stops you. That's normal — just wait for their reply, or remove them if you'd rather not include them.

Remember: a member who hasn't accepted yet is not included in the payout order, and the circle cannot be activated while they're still pending.
6

How a member accepts an invitation

When the invited member opens their app, the invitation appears on their home page, along with the circle's name and who invited them. They press "Accept" to join, or "Decline" to opt out.

7

Setting the monthly payout order

From the "Payout Order" tab you can see each member's turn to receive the pooled amount each month. Use the up/down arrows to reorder members however suits your group.

8

Activate the circle

Once everyone has accepted and the order looks right, press "Activate Circle" then confirm. From this moment the circle actually begins — and the payout order can no longer be changed.

Important: once activated, the payout order becomes permanent and cannot be edited. Double-check it before pressing "Confirm".
Part 2

This month's payments

Here we follow a different circle (with 4 members, running for a couple of months already) to see how payments get recorded, and how a member's self-reported payment gets approved or rejected.

9

The "Current Cycle" tab

This tab shows the current month: who's due to receive the pool this month, how much has been collected from members so far, and each member's payment status.

10

Recording a payment for a member

Next to any member's name press "Record Payment". Enter the amount they actually paid — it doesn't have to be the full contribution, you can record part of it now and the rest later.

Partial payments are fine: you can record more than one payment for the same member in the same month, until the contribution is complete.
11

When a member reports their own payment

The organizer doesn't have to record every payment — any member can press "Record my payment", enter the amount they paid, and attach proof (like a screenshot of a bank transfer), then submit the report.

This report isn't automatically accepted — it stays "pending" until the organizer reviews it.

12

Rejecting an unclear payment

The organizer opens the pending report and sees the amount and attached evidence. If something's wrong — say, the evidence is unclear — they press "Reject" and write a reason so the member knows what's needed.

Note: at any time, the member or organizer can click the "Rejected" or "Approved" badge next to a member's name to see that payment's details again.
13

Approving a valid payment

The member can submit a new report with clearer evidence. This time, the organizer opens the report and presses "Approve" — the amount is then automatically counted toward what's been collected.

14

The month is fully collected

Once every member has paid (whether recorded by the organizer or via an approved report), the month switches to "Collected" status — meaning the full required amount has been gathered.

Part 3

Paying out the recipient

Once collection is complete, the last step is handing the pooled amount to this month's recipient, and documenting it.

15

Confirming receipt — partial then full

Press "Confirm recipient's receipt". You can hand it over in two installments too: first part of it with evidence (like a transfer screenshot), then the rest later until the full amount is delivered.

Part 4

The full monthly record

The "Monthly Cycles" tab shows every month in the circle — past, current, and upcoming — with its full details.

16

Seeing every month at once

From the "Monthly Cycles" tab you can see every month in order, who paid how much, and even rejected and approved payments. This is the complete record of every financial move in the circle.

17

When a past month isn't fully settled

Sometimes a month ends and a member still hasn't paid their share. This doesn't stop the circle — that old month stays open, so you can record their late payment at any point afterward.

No problem if someone's late: you can always go back to any earlier month and record a late payment for it, even months afterward.
18

Recording a late payment

Next to the late member's name in that old month, press "Record Late Payment" and enter the amount. It's applied directly to that specific month.

19

Paying ahead for a future month

The opposite works too — if a member wants to pay their share for a month that hasn't arrived yet, that can be recorded the same way, using the "Record Advance Payment" button next to their name in that upcoming month.

20

Choosing a different month right from "Current Cycle"

Even from the "Current Cycle" tab itself, when recording a payment for a member you'll find a small link labeled "Record the amount on: Current Cycle" — click it and every other month (past or future) appears, so you can pick one directly without going to the "Monthly Cycles" tab at all.

Part 5

My account

Edit your name and phone number at any time.

21

Editing your name and phone number

From your name icon at the top of the page, open "My Profile". Edit your name or phone number as you like, then press "Save".
